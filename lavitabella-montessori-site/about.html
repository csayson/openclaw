The Method

About the Montessori Method

Dr. Maria Montessori, an Italian physician, built her method on scientific observation of how children actually develop.

Follow the Child

Lessons match your child’s interests and readiness — never a fixed pace imposed from outside.

The Prepared Environment

A beautiful, ordered space designed for self-discovery, where everything has its place and purpose.

Freedom & Independence

“Help me to do it myself.” Children build genuine confidence by doing real things on their own.

The Prepared Guide

Observation, understanding, and deep respect — the guide follows the child and presents the next step at the right moment.

“I have studied the child… and that is what is called the Montessori Method.” — Maria Montessori
