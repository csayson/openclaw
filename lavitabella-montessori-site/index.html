The Problem

Overcrowded, inflexible schools

Most schools can’t give your child individual attention — or your family any flexibility. Rigid schedules and one-size-fits-all worksheets dim a child’s natural curiosity.

External

The quiet worry

“Am I giving my child the best possible start?” Homeschooling alone is exhausting, and the doubt never fully goes away.

Internal

What children deserve

Children should be treated as capable individuals — not managed in a crowd.

Children who don’t get individualized attention in their early years often come to believe that learning is hard or boring — a belief that can follow them for life. The 3–6 window is uniquely powerful. We don’t think you should leave it to chance.
