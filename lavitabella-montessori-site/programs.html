Frequently Asked

What is a hybrid Montessori school?

A hybrid Montessori school combines the depth of an authentic Montessori classroom with the flexibility of a part-time schedule. Your child attends 2–3 mornings a week with a trained guide, and you keep the rest of the week for home learning, family, and life. It’s the expertise of a real school plus the freedom of homeschooling.

Is this a good fit for homeschooling families?

Yes — it’s designed with you in mind. Many of our families homeschool and use us for expert Montessori instruction, social connection, and hands-on enrichment without giving up the flexibility they love.

What does AMI-certified mean and why does it matter?

AMI is the Association Montessori Internationale, founded by Dr. Maria Montessori herself. An AMI-trained guide has completed rigorous training in how children actually develop and how to present Montessori materials with precision — so your child gets the real method, not a watered-down version.

What does a typical day look like?

Children arrive to a calm, prepared environment and settle into a long, uninterrupted work cycle, choosing meaningful activities and receiving individual lessons from the guide. The day blends math, language, sensorial, practical life, and cultural work, with time outdoors. OWNER: confirm and expand the daily rhythm.
